## 👩‍💻 About Me

Hi, I'm **Nada Bassam Almoqayad**, a Software Engineer who graduated with an **Excellent** grade, focused on backend development with **Laravel**.

- 🛠️ I build **RESTful APIs** and server-rendered web apps with **PHP, Laravel, Blade and MySQL**.
- 🎨 I also have front-end experience, so I can take a feature from the database all the way to the screen.
- 🧩 I've worked on several projects: a clinic management system, a smart restaurant menu, a blogging platform, and a task manager.
- 💼 As part of a development team at a company, I worked on a social platform, an e-commerce store, SaaS dashboards, and the company's services website.
- 🏥 I trained in the IT department of the Palestinian Ministry of Health, where I developed internal websites.
- 🔐 I write code with a **security mindset**: validating input, controlling who can access what, and thinking about how a feature could be misused before it ships.
- 🌱 Fast learner, always improving how I structure and test my code.
